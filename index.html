VESPID

Public Status: Escaped Lab Experiment

The Backstory

The corporation called it gene-splicing research. He called it a cage. Subjected to illegal cross-breeding trials combining wasp and tiger beetle DNA, he lived through months of agonizing cellular restructuring.


When the facility security protocol failed during a midnight blackout, he didn't run to save the world—he broke out to burn the lab to the ground. Left with animal instincts, hybrid biology, and a deep grudge against the system, he chose the streets over captivity.

The Anti-Hero

The media paints him as a dangerous monster on the loose, and the police treat him as public enemy number one. But he operates in the dark gray areas of justice—protecting the helpless by dealing with the scum the law can't touch, entirely on his own brutal terms.


Powers & Weaknesses:

  • Wasp & Tiger Beetle DNA: Insane sprinting speeds and devastating mandibles.
  • Venom Spit: Paralyzes targets on impact.
  • The Blind Spot: Extreme speeds make him temporarily blind.
  • Thermal Vulnerability: Weak to low temperatures, countered by a warm, moth-inspired thermal cape.
