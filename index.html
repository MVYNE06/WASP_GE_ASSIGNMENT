VESPID: The Enforcer

Justice has a new sting.

The Origin

Before the mask, there was just an entomology lab, a highly illegal genetic sequencer, and a swarm of mutated Vespidae. When a rogue syndicate attempted to steal the research, one scientist was caught in the crossfire of shattered glass and experimental neuro-toxins.


They thought he was dead. Instead, the venom rewrote his DNA. Now, he patrols the neon-lit alleyways of the city, hunting those who prey on the weak.

Vow of Justice

The city's underworld is a hive of corruption, but Vespid answers to a higher moral law. He stands as the ultimate arbiter of justice, wrapping his ruthless methods in a strict code: he only strikes those who have poisoned society.


Abilities:

  • Neuro-Venom Spit: A highly corrosive, localized projectile that melts through armor and weapons.
  • Paralytic Sting: A close-combat strike that locks the victim's nervous system for 12 hours. Zero casualties. Maximum justice.
