VESPID

Public Status: Escaped Lab Experiment

The Backstory

The lab called it gene splicing research. He called it a cage. They forced him through illegal cross breeding trials using wasp and tiger beetle DNA. He survived months of pure agony as his cells mutated.


When the power grid failed during a storm he didn't stick around to save the world. He broke out to burn the lab down. Now he has wild animal instincts a hybrid body and a deep grudge against the system. He chose the streets over a cage.

The Anti Hero

The media paints him as a dangerous monster and the cops treat him as public enemy number one. But he operates in the dark gray areas of justice. He protects the helpless by dealing with the scum the law ignores doing it entirely on his own brutal terms.


Powers and Weaknesses:

  • Wasp and Tiger Beetle DNA: Insane sprinting speeds and devastating mandibles.
  • Venom Spit: Paralyzes targets on impact.
  • The Blind Spot: Extreme speeds make him temporarily blind.
  • Thermal Vulnerability: Weak to low temperatures. This is countered by a warm moth inspired thermal cape.
